Welcome to Sync Point!

This is a cooperative music synthesiser for two players. Player A must tap out a steady rhythm continuously to build up the ring’s progress. Player B must follow the moving target; you’ll find that different positions on the grid affect the left and right channels as well as the pitch of the sound.

Keep going for 10 seconds to succeed! If you’ve forgotten how to play, feel free to come back and check this guide at any time.
